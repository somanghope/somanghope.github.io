Hi, I'm **Somang Kim (김소망)**, a Ph.D. student in the [Department of Geography and the Environment](https://liberalarts.utexas.edu/geography/) at **The University of Texas at Austin**, where I work in the [GISense Lab](https://sites.utexas.edu/gisense/) with Dr. Yuhao Kang.

I study how people **experience places** — what we see, feel, and imagine when we look at a street or a landscape. My work brings together **GeoAI**, **street view imagery**, and **brain imaging** (fMRI, MEG, fNIRS) to measure those subjective experiences and to ask how the brain represents geographic environments. I call this direction **neuro-geography**.

Before coming to Austin, I earned my M.A. in Geography and B.A. in Geography Education (minor in Computer Science and Engineering) at Ewha Womans University in Seoul, where I used deep learning on street view images to model perceived fear of crime and walkability.
